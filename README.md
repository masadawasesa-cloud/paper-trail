**Paper Trail — Night at the Ministry.** A top-down stealth game about stealing the truth. A corrupt minister keeps his crimes in three books hidden on three floors of his tower: the procurement ledger, the offshore accounts file, and a black book in his own hand. You are the junior clerk he never noticed. Slip past patrolling guards and their torch cones, hide behind archive shelves and inside the planter shrubs, tiptoe so your steps make no sound, lift all three books and reach the roof before dawn — the papers must reach the press.

Guards patrol learnable routes, pause to scan, investigate noises, grow suspicious, give chase, then search and give up — the SPOTTED meter fills as you are recognised and drains while you stay hidden, so a near miss is a story, not a game over. Three floors: Records & Archives, the Open-Plan Office, and the Minister's Suite, where a sweeping camera watches the door.

Play it: serve the `game/` folder with any static file server (for example `node serve.ts` from the competition kit, or `python3 -m http.server` inside `game/`) and open `index.html` in a desktop browser. WASD or arrow keys move, SHIFT tiptoes. No build step, no network, no libraries — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with sound synthesised in WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
